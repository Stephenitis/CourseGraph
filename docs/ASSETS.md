# Unused building exploration assets

Building imagery was explored and subsequently removed at the user's request. No building photograph, sketch, caption, or credit disclosure currently renders in the app. The files below remain unused in the repository; this document preserves their provenance.

| Local asset | Subject and source | Attribution and license |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `web/public/images/buildings/stata-center.jpg` | [Stata Center](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stata_Center.JPG), explored for EECS context | Millylang; public domain |
| `web/public/images/buildings/simons-building.jpg` | [MIT Department of Mathematics](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MIT_-_Department_of_Mathematics_(54960639561).jpg), explored for mathematics context | Ajay Suresh; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

The photo experiment used cropping, desaturation, and a feathered fade. Department context came from [MIT EECS history](https://www.eecs.mit.edu/about/departmental-history/) and [MIT's Simons Building project](https://capitalprojects.mit.edu/projects/mathematics-building-2); neither source establishes where any individual class meets.

Earlier generated interpretations, `stata-sketch.png` and `simons-sketch.png`, and their generation metadata also remain in `web/public/images/buildings/`. The unused `ProgramBuilding` component is no longer imported or rendered by the app.

## Student profile placeholder

`web/public/images/student-profile-placeholder.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-23. It depicts a fictional undergraduate and is not a photo of Stephen or an authenticated user. The portrait appears in the map identity strip and the prototype account avatar.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square profile avatar for a university course planning prototype. Create one photorealistic head-and-shoulders portrait of a fictional 18-year-old male undergraduate, eager, thoughtful and pleasantly nerdy, with simple glasses, slightly tousled hair, a natural friendly smile, and a grey MIT sweatshirt with small muted cardinal MIT lettering. Soft natural daylight, softly blurred neutral campus background. Centered face large enough to read in a 56-pixel circular UI avatar, generous safe crop around hair and shoulders. Authentic student candid-photo feel, realistic skin texture, no beauty advertising gloss, no exaggerated expression, no props, no watermark. This is an invented placeholder character, not a likeness of any actual person.
